# Week 1 Quiz - Decorators, iterators, and generators

Answer without running the examples, then explain your reasoning.

1. What function call does `@decorate` above `def task(): ...` represent?
2. What problem does `functools.wraps` solve for a decorator?
3. What exception signals that an iterator has no next value?
4. What is the difference between an iterable and an iterator?
5. When does a generator function execute its body: when called, or when its iterator is advanced?
6. What happens when a generator is consumed completely and then iterated again?
7. Give one reason to use a generator and one situation where a list is a better choice.
8. Does `yield from values` build a list of `values` first?
