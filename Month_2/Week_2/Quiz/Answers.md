1. `{}` creates an empty dictionary; `set()` creates an empty set.
2. Duplicates are removed; each distinct hashable value appears once.
3. `|` is union; `&` is intersection.
4. It raises `KeyError`.
5. Use `record.get("missing", fallback)`.
6. Key-value pairs, each represented as a tuple.
7. No. Lists are mutable and therefore unhashable; dictionary keys must be hashable.
