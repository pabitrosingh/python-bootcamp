# Exercise 3 - Discount eligibility

Ask for an order total and whether the customer has a membership (`yes` or `no`). Give a 10% discount when the total is at least $50 and the customer is a member. Otherwise, keep the original total.

Display the discount and final total to two decimal places. Convert the membership answer to lowercase before comparing it.

**Test:** Check a member at $50, a member below $50, and a non-member above $50.
