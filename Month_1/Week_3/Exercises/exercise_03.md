# Exercise 3 - FizzBuzz

Print the numbers from 1 through 30. For multiples of 3 print `Fizz`, for multiples of 5 print `Buzz`, and for multiples of both print `FizzBuzz`.

**Hint:** Check divisibility by both 3 and 5 before checking either one alone.

**Test:** The output for 15 should be `FizzBuzz`, not just `Fizz`.
