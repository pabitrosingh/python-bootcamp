# Exercise 3 - Refactor a grade calculator

Write three functions:

1. `calculate_average(scores)` returns the arithmetic mean of a non-empty list of scores.
2. `letter_grade(average)` returns A, B, C, D, or F using the Week 2 scale.
3. `print_report(name, average, grade)` displays a readable report.

Use the functions together for at least two students. Keep calculations separate from display.

**Test:** Scores `[80, 90, 100]` should produce an average of `90` and grade `A`.
