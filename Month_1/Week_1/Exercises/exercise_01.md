# Exercise 1 - About me

Create `about_me.py`.

1. Store your name, a subject you enjoy, and the number of hours you want to study this week in variables.
2. Print each value on its own line.
3. Print their types using `type()`.

**Requirements**

- Use at least one `str`, one `int`, and one `bool`.
- Use descriptive variable names.
- Do not put quotation marks around numeric or Boolean values.

**Self-check:** The name and subject should be strings, the hours should be an integer, and the Boolean should display as `True` or `False`.
