# Exercise 2 - Shopping total

Create `shopping_total.py`. Ask for the name of an item, its price, and how many were purchased. Convert the price to a `float` and the quantity to an `int`, then display the item and total cost to two decimal places.

Example interaction:

```text
Item: notebook
Price: 3.50
Quantity: 4
4 notebook(s) cost $14.00.
```

**Requirements**

- Use `input()` for all three values.
- Convert both numeric inputs before calculating.
- Use an f-string and `:.2f` to format the total.
