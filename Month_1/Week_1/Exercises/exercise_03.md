# Exercise 3 - Personal introduction

Create `introduction.py`. Ask for a person's name, city, and age in whole years. Calculate their age in five years, then print a friendly two-sentence introduction.

**Requirements**

- Convert the age to an integer.
- Remove accidental spaces at the beginning or end of the name and city with `.strip()`.
- Use f-strings for the output.

**Try next:** Ask how many years into the future to calculate, then convert that answer to an integer too.
